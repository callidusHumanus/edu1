The role

What a beta tester does

Run experiments

Work through virtual chemistry, physics and biology labs the way a student would.

Check the science

Spot wrong formulas, units, labels and results, and tell us what the correct answer is.

Report bugs clearly

Write short, precise reports with the steps that caused the problem.

Suggest improvements

Tell us what would make a lab clearer, more fun and more useful for your classmates.
